Open the terminal

This is a full terminal on your server. It has its own long password, kept on the server. To see it, run cat ~/.jack-terminal-password in your SSH app, then paste it here.

How to use this

  • Your chatType jackchat and press return, or choose “Open my Claude chat” in the menu. It keeps running when you close this page.
  • CopyPress and hold the screen to select text with your phone's own selector, then use its Copy. Tap a link to open it. The Copy button up top shows everything as plain text, including what scrolled away. On a computer, select text and press Ctrl/⌘-C.
  • WindowsThe tabs at the top are your windows. Tap one to switch, + for another, \u00d7 to close the one you are in, or swipe the screen sideways. They all keep running on the server.
  • WriteOn a phone, tap the screen or the pencil for a message box where suggestions and autocorrect work. Send with nothing in it is the return key. The keyboard key types straight into the terminal instead.
  • SpeakTap the microphone. Your words appear in a box you can correct, then Send puts them in the terminal.
  • PhotosTap the camera to take or choose a picture. It is saved on your server and its location is typed into your message.
  • Scroll backSwipe up and down on the terminal, or use the page keys.
  • esc, ctrlTap esc to interrupt Claude. Tap ctrl, then a letter, for a control key; ^C is on its own key.
  • Windows“New window” in the menu opens a second shell beside your chat. Tabs then appear under the title: tap one to switch, tap + for another. Type exit to close one.
  • As an appChoose “Add to Home Screen” in the menu. It then opens full screen from its own icon and stays signed in.
