Open the terminal

This is a full terminal on your server. It has its own long password, kept on the server. To see it, run cat ~/.jack-terminal-password in your SSH app, then paste it here.

How to use this

  • Your chatType jackchat and press return, or choose “Open my Claude chat” in the menu. It keeps running when you close this page.
  • SpeakTap the microphone. Your words appear in a box you can correct, then Send puts them in the terminal.
  • PhotosTap the camera to take or choose a picture. It is saved on your server and its location is typed into your message.
  • Scroll backSwipe up and down on the terminal, or use the page keys.
  • esc, ctrlTap esc to interrupt Claude. Tap ctrl, then a letter, for a control key; ^C is on its own key.
  • Windows“New window” in the menu opens a second shell beside your chat; a strip along the bottom shows which one you are in.
  • As an appChoose “Add to Home Screen” in the menu. It then opens full screen from its own icon and stays signed in.
