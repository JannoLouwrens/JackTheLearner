## A rule I changed, and in which direction

The rule for choosing the brain said "he averages 5 meals a life". The world
test showed 5 meals is a starving life. The mark is now **a sustained life:
he reaches the end of the life and eats at least what he burns.** Stricter,
never looser. The 5-meal count is still reported. I also chose the starting
rung after seeing its tables; of the two settings that qualified I took the
harder, and the ruling says so in the open.
