## 3b. Apparatus arithmetic on the partial (11-19 12:45; 12/40 cells; reported, NOT §4's reading)
Why: at 12/40 every learner arm ends below its never-trained twin K-lr0 (INBOX 12:30). This section prices one candidate cause from settings and logged lives only; no run was made, and nothing here changes the rule or the arms.
- Settings (`pilot_b.make_b`): SBX SAC, **ent_coef 0.1 fixed** (no target entropy), γ 0.995, warm-up 5,000, batch 256. Action dims **8** (measured: `pilot_b.make_world(402).action_space` = Box(-1, 1, (8,))).
- Entropy-bonus ceiling: a policy on [-1, 1]^8 has entropy at most 8·ln 2 = 5.55 nats, so SAC's per-decision bonus −α·log π is **≤ 0.555**.
- Task reward per decision, from the logged lives of p0–p2 (6 arms × 2 cells): mean K −0.023, K-lr0 −0.008, B0 −0.032, G −0.033, P +0.011, KP +0.013; meal-less lives' median K −0.036. K-lr0's meals are worth 100 × 1.03 / 3,371 = **+0.031 per decision**.
- So at the ceiling the entropy term is ~15× the drain and ~18× K-lr0's meal income per decision. At γ 0.995 it is worth up to 0.555/(1−γ) ≈ **111**, about one meal (+100), in every state, food or not.
